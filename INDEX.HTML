Week 0 - Forge Orientation & Team Building

Overview

Week 0 marked the beginning of my ProtoSem journey through the Forge Accelerator Orientation Program. The orientation introduced the program objectives, learning approach, and the importance of continuous learning, teamwork, and professional development. During the session, we explored Bruce Lee's philosophy through the Zen Pencils (Kamiks) comic, which shared the message, "Absorb what is useful, reject what is useless, and add what is uniquely your own." This inspired me to learn from different people, adopt useful knowledge, ignore what is unnecessary, and develop my own unique approach towards learning. We also participated in the Marshmallow Tower Challenge, where teamwork, communication, planning, and creativity were more important than competition. Finally, I completed the 16 Personalities Assessment, which identified my personality type as Advocate (INFJ-T) and helped me understand my strengths and areas for improvement.

Objectives

  • Understand the Forge Orientation Program.
  • Develop teamwork and collaboration.
  • Improve communication and creative thinking.
  • Learn Bruce Lee's philosophy of continuous learning.
  • Identify personal strengths through personality assessment.
