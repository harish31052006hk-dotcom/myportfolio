PROTOSEM Week 05 Completed

Understanding Users, Problems & Research

Overview

Week 5 focused on understanding users, exploring a new problem statement, and learning the fundamentals of user research and design thinking. During this week, I was introduced to UI/UX design concepts, focusing on how interfaces should be built around genuine user needs and ease of use.


We were also introduced to a new problem statement: Digital Services and Tracking in Local Bus. To better understand this domain and user challenges in public transportation, we prepared and conducted a user survey. In addition, we engaged in the Alpha Team collaborative activity and practiced structured documentation through the Mentor of Meeting (MoM) process.


Alongside these activities, I started an initial literature review for my core ProtoSem project: Non-invasive methane monitoring for cattle and livestock, exploring existing research, current approaches, and identifying areas for further investigation.

Objectives

  • Understand the basics of UI/UX and user-centered design.
  • Understand the Digital Services and Tracking in Local Bus problem statement.
  • Learn how surveys can help understand user needs.
  • Improve teamwork and documentation through Alpha Team and MoM activities.
  • Understand existing research related to non-invasive methane monitoring for cattle and livestock.

Activities

UI/UX Learning

I was introduced to the fundamentals of UI/UX design. I learned how good interfaces focus on user needs, usability, clear navigation, and simple interface layouts. This session helped me understand how users interact with digital products and why creating a seamless user experience is essential before jumping into technical implementation.

Problem Statement – Digital Services and Tracking in Local Bus

A new problem statement was given to us: Digital Services and Tracking in Local Bus. The focus of this activity was to analyze and understand the daily transportation challenges passengers face, and how digital services and tracking can help improve the local bus travel experience.

User Survey

To better understand the problem statement, we prepared and conducted a user survey. The survey helped us gather valuable insights, understand commuter habits, and identify the practical difficulties and expectations people have regarding local bus timing, tracking, and information access.

Alpha Team Activity

I participated in the Alpha Team activity as part of our ProtoSem collaborative learning. This exercise gave us an opportunity to practice group discussions, exchange ideas, and understand how different team members collaborate to address challenge scenarios.

Mentor of Meeting (MoM)

We worked on documenting the Mentor of Meeting (MoM). Preparing and writing the MoM helped us systematically record key discussion points, mentor inputs, and team action items, improving our project tracking and professional documentation skills.

Literature Review – Non-invasive Methane Monitoring

I started an initial literature review on our core project topic: Non-invasive methane monitoring for cattle and livestock. I explored existing literature to understand current approaches, existing technologies, how methane measurement is carried out, and where potential gaps exist for further exploration.

  • Understood the importance of gathering user information before developing a solution.
  • Explored the Digital Services and Tracking in Local Bus problem statement through a survey.
  • Improved teamwork and documentation through Alpha Team and MoM activities.
  • Started understanding existing research related to non-invasive methane monitoring for cattle and livestock.

Reflection

Week 5 helped me realize that solving any problem effectively starts with truly understanding the users and the core problem itself. Learning the basics of UI/UX taught me to view things from a user's perspective, prioritizing usability and simplicity.


Conducting the survey for the local bus tracking problem statement demonstrated how important it is to collect real feedback before jumping to solutions. Furthermore, the Alpha Team activity and MoM documentation reinforced the value of clear teamwork and structured records. Finally, beginning the literature review gave me an initial foundation on existing work around non-invasive methane monitoring for livestock.
