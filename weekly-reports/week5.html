Week 5 – UI/UX, Problem Discovery & Research

Overview

Week 5 helped me understand how UI/UX, user needs, problem discovery, teamwork, and research are connected. During this week, I was introduced to UI/UX concepts and worked around the problem statement Digital Services and Tracking in Local Bus. A survey was conducted to understand the problem and gather user-related information to look at the challenges from a commuter's perspective.


I also participated in Alpha Team activities and worked on a Mentor of Meeting (MoM), which helped me understand teamwork, discussion, and documentation. Alongside this, I started a literature review related to my team's problem statement: Non-invasive methane monitoring for cattle and livestock, exploring existing work to gain foundational context before moving forward.

Key Learnings

🎨

UI/UX Fundamentals

Learned the basic ideas of UI/UX and how user needs and usability are considered while designing a product.

🔍

User Discovery

Learned the importance of understanding users and their needs before thinking about a solution.

🚌

Problem Statement

Explored the problem statement Digital Services and Tracking in Local Bus and started understanding the problem from a user perspective.

📋

Survey & Requirement Understanding

Participated in a survey to collect information and understand the needs and experiences related to the problem statement.

🤝

Teamwork & MoM

Participated in Alpha Team activities and worked on a Mentor of Meeting (MoM), helping me understand teamwork, discussion, and proper documentation.

📚

Literature Review

Started reviewing existing information and research related to non-invasive methane monitoring for cattle and livestock to understand the existing work around our team's problem.

Reflection

This week helped me understand that solving a problem starts with understanding the users and the problem clearly. The survey helped me see the importance of collecting information before deciding on a solution. The literature review also showed me why it is important to understand what has already been done before developing a new approach. Working with the Alpha Team and drafting the MoM reminded me that good communication and disciplined documentation are just as vital as technical ideas.
