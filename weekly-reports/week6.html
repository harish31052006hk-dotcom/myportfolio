Week 6 – Embedded Systems, Soldering & Game Development

Overview

Week 6 helped me understand the basics of microcontrollers, microprocessors, and embedded systems. I learned how embedded systems are structured and how sensors and actuators interact with the physical environment. I also gained hands-on experience in soldering and desoldering electronic components and learned how basic electronic circuits are assembled.


In addition, I worked on an ESP32-Based Multiplayer Pong Game with OLED Display and Laptop Keyboard Control. This activity introduced me to the practical application of embedded systems, display interfacing, and game logic development using the ESP32 platform. Working on the project helped me understand how hardware and software can be combined to create an interactive system.

Key Learnings

🧠

Microcontrollers & Microprocessors

Learned the basic concepts of microcontrollers and microprocessors and their role in electronic and embedded systems.

🏗️

Embedded Systems

Understood the basic structure of embedded systems and how they are used in real-world applications.

📡

Sensors & Actuators

Learned how sensors and actuators interact with the physical environment and contribute to embedded system functionality.

🔥

Soldering & Desoldering

Gained hands-on experience in soldering and desoldering electronic components and learned the importance of proper circuit connections.

🔌

Circuit Assembly

Learned how basic electronic components are connected and assembled to form a working circuit.

⏱️

555 Timer Circuit

Practiced assembling and soldering a 555 timer circuit using LEDs, resistors, and a capacitor, which improved my understanding of electronic circuits and hardware assembly.

🎮

ESP32 Multiplayer Pong Game

Developed an ESP32-Based Multiplayer Pong Game with OLED Display and Laptop Keyboard Control. This activity helped me understand display interfacing, basic game logic, serial communication, and how embedded systems can be used for interactive applications.

Reflection

This week gave me a better understanding of how embedded systems combine hardware and software to create functional applications. Learning about microcontrollers, embedded architecture, and electronic circuits strengthened my technical foundation. The soldering activities improved my confidence in handling electronic components and assembling circuits. Working on the ESP32 Multiplayer Pong Game showed me how programming, displays, and microcontrollers can work together to create an interactive system, making the learning experience both practical and engaging.
