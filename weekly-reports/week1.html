PROTOSEM Week 01 Completed

5S Implementation

Overview

Week 1 focused on the 5S Implementation activity. I worked as a member of the Battery Team, where my responsibility was to inspect and organize battery adapters. This activity helped me understand the importance of maintaining a clean, organized, and efficient workplace while following the principles of 5S. It also strengthened my teamwork, responsibility, and workplace discipline.

Objectives

  • Understand the 5S workplace methodology.
  • Improve teamwork and collaboration.
  • Learn workplace organization.
  • Maintain a clean and organized workspace.

Activities

  • 5S Implementation
  • Battery Team Assignment
  • Battery Adapter Inspection
  • Organizing Battery Adapters
  • Workplace Organization

Challenges

Carefully inspecting every battery adapter and organizing them correctly while coordinating with teammates within the given time.

Skills Learned

Teamwork Communication Workplace Organization Attention to Detail Responsibility

Concepts Learned

5S Methodology Workplace Organization Teamwork Battery Management

Key Learnings

  • Understood the importance of the 5S methodology.
  • Learned how workplace organization improves efficiency.
  • Improved teamwork and communication skills.
  • Gained practical experience in organizing workplace materials.

Reflection

Implementing 5S systematically reduces task cycle times and eliminates inventory waste, creating an optimal baseline for modern DevOps and agile project management workflows.

Conclusion

Week 1 successfully established standard operating practices for physical and operational organization within project team environments.
