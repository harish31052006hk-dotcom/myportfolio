PROTOSEM Week 00 Completed

Forge Orientation & Team Building

Overview

Week 0 marked the beginning of my ProtoSem journey through the Forge Accelerator Orientation Program. The orientation introduced the program objectives, learning approach, and the importance of continuous learning, teamwork, and professional development.


During the session, we explored Bruce Lee's philosophy through the Zen Pencils (Kamiks) comic, which shared the message, "Absorb what is useful, reject what is useless, and add what is uniquely your own." This inspired me to learn from different people, adopt useful knowledge, ignore what is unnecessary, and develop my own unique approach towards learning.


We also participated in the Marshmallow Tower Challenge, where teamwork, communication, planning, and creativity were more important than competition.


Finally, I completed the 16 Personalities Assessment, which identified my personality type as Advocate (INFJ-T) and helped me understand my strengths and areas for improvement.

Objectives

  • Understand the Forge Orientation Program.
  • Develop teamwork and collaboration.
  • Improve communication and creative thinking.
  • Learn Bruce Lee's philosophy of continuous learning.
  • Identify personal strengths through personality assessment.

Activities

  • Forge Accelerator Orientation
  • Zen Pencils (Bruce Lee Philosophy)
  • Marshmallow Tower Challenge
  • 16 Personalities Assessment

Challenges

Designing a stable marshmallow tower within a limited time required effective teamwork, communication, planning, and quick decision-making. The activity demonstrated that collaboration is more important than individual effort.

Skills Learned

Teamwork Communication Problem Solving Critical Thinking Creativity Leadership Adaptability Collaboration

Concepts Learned

Forge Accelerator Orientation Bruce Lee Philosophy (Zen Pencils) Growth Mindset 16 Personalities Assessment Team Building

Key Learnings

  • Understood the ProtoSem learning approach.
  • Improved teamwork and collaboration.
  • Learned Bruce Lee's philosophy of selective learning.
  • Improved communication and leadership.
  • Identified my personality type as Advocate (INFJ-T).

Reflection

The orientation phase laid a fundamental mindset foundation. Understanding personality dynamics within a team helps in delegating roles effectively and fostering an inclusive environment for innovation.

Conclusion

Week 0 successfully established the mindset, behavioral expectations, and collaborative framework necessary for navigating the intense engineering and technical curriculum of Protosem.
